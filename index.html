The bottom line

The degree helps. Experience decides the timing.

Across the postings, the MURP is valuable—but it usually complements municipal experience rather than replacing it. One continuous internship or assistant role is the cleanest bridge to Planner I.

1Entry work can begin during school.

Intern, hourly planning assistant and technician roles range from no experience to roughly one year. Coursework, GIS samples and public-facing work matter here.

2Planner I usually means a bachelor’s plus one year.

Littleton is more flexible at six months; Englewood starts at zero. A four-month internship alone will not satisfy every one-year screen.

3Planner II is normally the first post-master’s promotion.

The recurring threshold is about three years. Loveland and Louisville explicitly give a completed master’s more weight than most employers.
